Tap a card to pick up its run, then tap where it should go. Drag works too. Arrow keys move around the board, Enter picks up or drops, A deals from the stock, U undoes and H hints.

Game details

How to play

Build every column downwards, in any suit. A run of cards in one suit, descending, can be picked up and moved together — a run of thirteen from king down to ace is lifted off the board, and eight of those win the game. The stock deals one card to every column, but only while no column is empty. When the stock runs out, there is nothing left to turn over: the board is all there is.
